Delete your ResortPro account

How to delete your account and what happens to your data

Option 1: Delete in the app

  1. Open the ResortPro app and sign in.
  2. Go to Account.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete account.
  4. Read the warning, wait for the 3-second countdown, then tap Delete to confirm.

If you have an upcoming active booking, you need to cancel it or wait until it is finished before you can delete your account.

Option 2: Request by email

If you can no longer open the app, send an email to aryanasguesthouse06@gmail.com with the subject "ResortPro account deletion request". Send it from the Google account (email address) you used to sign in to ResortPro. We will process your request within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in record
  • Your profile information (username, profile picture, verification details)
  • Your notifications

What is kept

Records of your past bookings and payments are kept, under your name but no longer linked to any account, because the resort needs them for accounting and legal purposes. These records are only visible to authorized resort staff.
