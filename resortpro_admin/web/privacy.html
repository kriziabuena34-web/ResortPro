ResortPro Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Aryana's Guests House ("we", "us") collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal data when you use the ResortPro mobile app ("the App") to book a stay at our resort in Brgy. Maulawin, Pagsanjan, Laguna, Philippines. We process personal data in accordance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and the rules of the National Privacy Commission (NPC).

1. Information we collect

  • Google account information: your name, email address, and profile photo, received when you sign in with Google.
  • Profile information: the username and profile picture you choose in the App.
  • Identity verification: your full name, birth date, mobile number, government ID type, and a photo of your government ID, which you submit to verify your identity before booking.
  • Booking information: your chosen package, booking date, number and age group of guests, and event type (if any).
  • Payment records: the amount, method (GCash or Maya), reference number, status, and the screenshot you upload as proof of payment. We never collect your GCash or Maya PIN, password, or card numbers.
  • Location (optional): if you allow it, the App reads your device's location to show you on the resort map and to draw a route to the resort. Your location is not saved in our database.
  • Notifications: booking and payment updates that we send to your account.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and verify your identity.
  • To process, confirm, and manage your bookings.
  • To verify your payments and issue receipts.
  • To send you updates about your booking and payments.
  • To let resort staff contact you about your reservation.
  • To keep business records as required by law.

3. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored with Supabase, a cloud database provider, protected by access-control rules so that you can only see your own records. Your government ID photo and payment screenshots are kept in private storage that only you and authorized resort staff can open. Profile pictures are stored in public storage so they can be displayed in the App.

4. Third-party services

The App uses the following services, each with its own privacy policy:

  • Google Sign-In to log you in.
  • Supabase to store your account, bookings, payments, and files.
  • OpenStreetMap to display map tiles.
  • OSRM (router.project-osrm.org) to calculate the driving route to the resort. When you ask for directions, your current coordinates are sent to this service to compute the route.
  • Google Maps, only if you tap "Get directions", which opens the Google Maps app or website.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. Only authorized staff of Aryana's Guests House can view your information, and only to verify your identity and manage your bookings and payments. We may disclose data when required by law.

6. Data retention

We keep your account data while your account is active. If you delete your account, your profile, username, profile picture, and notifications are removed. Records of past bookings and payments are kept under your name, without a link to your account, because the resort needs them for accounting and legal purposes.

7. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act, you have the right to be informed, to access your data, to correct inaccurate data, to object to processing, to request deletion or blocking of your data, and to file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

You can delete your account at any time. See how to delete your account.

8. Children

The App is intended for users who are 18 years old or older. Bookings for minors must be made by a parent or guardian.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. When we do, we will change the effective date above and, for important changes, notify you in the App.
